People are greedy with life, in the best way: the work, the weekends, the late nights, the people we love. But bodies have limits. A long week catches up. A bad night lingers. The small physical costs of living fully start to add up, and somewhere along the way you start making trade-offs you never signed up for.

Songo takes those costs off the table, across the way you sit, sleep, and move. It works in the background, so you feel it in how your days go, not in one more thing to manage. We notice before you do, and don't make a thing of it. Your body doesn't need more interruptions. It needs more support.

## What we're building

### Songo SIT°

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Launching-2026-006934?style=flat-square&labelColor=DAFF00" alt="Launching 2026"/>
